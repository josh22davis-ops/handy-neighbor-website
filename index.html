How a project goes.

Four steps from your first call to the finished project. Same process whether it’s a small repair or a basement finish-out.

Step 1

Free On-Site Estimate

Call, text, or fill out the form. I come walk the project with you, listen to what you have in mind, and talk through what’s realistic for the scope and the season.

Step 2

Written Quote

A flat-rate written quote in your hands before any work starts — scope, materials, and timeline spelled out. No hourly billing. If something unexpected turns up mid-project, I stop and walk you through the options first.

Scheduled & Started

We pick a date that works for both of us. For larger projects, the deposit is 50% to start and 50% on completion. Smaller jobs can be paid in full when finished.

  • ★ Owner-operated — me on every visit

Done Right & Cleaned Up

Project finished, site cleaned, tools off your driveway. Photos of the work for your records. 50% balance due on completion. Then a quick walk-through so you can see everything.

  • ★ Final walk-through together
  • ★ Photos of the work for your records

  • ★ Standing by for the next thing on your list

About Your Neighbor

Josh Davis — Owner & Operator

The Handy Neighbor Co. started the way most good ideas do — a neighbor needed help with something small, and I was the guy who actually showed up.

Insured with over 20 years of hands-on experience — building, fixing, and taking care of homes. From flooring and trim to plumbing, electrical, windows, and installs — the kind of work that teaches you what lasts, what doesn’t, and how to do it right the first time.

These days, the big crews and long timelines are behind. Because in every house, there’s always a list. Things to fix. Things to finish. Things that got pushed to “next weekend” three weekends ago. Not just the big projects — but the ones that get overlooked. The dryer vent that needs cleared. The gutters filling up. The air filter that should’ve been changed weeks ago.

The bigger projects and the small fixes that keep a home safe, clean, and running the way it should. Turns out, the neighbors needed the same thing.

The neighbor down the street — the one who shows up, takes care of it, and does it right. No crews. No runaround. No overcomplicating it. Just solid, reliable work — done right, with care.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of projects do you take on? +

A wide range. On the exterior: wood rot and siding repair, roofing, gutter guards, entry doors, exterior lighting, concrete crack sealing, deck staining and repair, and outdoor patios with natural gas fire features. On the interior: basement finish-outs, bathroom updates, mudroom and entryway built-ins, closet and pantry build-outs, garage storage systems, tile, flooring, and trim work. And the dozen small projects you've been putting off. If you're not sure whether I do it, just ask.

How does pricing work? +

Every project starts with a free on-site estimate. I walk the project with you, scope what's involved, and put a flat-rate written quote in your hands before any work starts. No hourly billing. If something unexpected turns up once the work is underway, I stop, lay out all the options, and we pick the best path together — nothing extra gets done without your okay. Materials are billed separately. For larger projects, the deposit is 50% to start and 50% on completion.

Who does the work? +

Josh Davis — owner, operator, and the only person who shows up. Insured with 20+ years of hands-on experience in residential construction and commercial facility management. No subcontractors. No strangers. Just a neighbor with the right tools and the know-how to use them. For specialty work that needs a state license (HVAC, full plumbing, electrical beyond fixture swaps), I bring in trusted licensed partners.

What neighborhoods do you serve? +

The Ridges first, then Shadow Ridge, Armbrust Acres, Bay Shores, Leawood, and Pacific Park in West Omaha. The Ridges is the priority neighborhood — same Ridges pricing for anyone in those surrounding areas. If your neighborhood isn’t listed, reach out anyway — areas with the most interest get added first.

What does the “best value” positioning mean? +

It means you’re getting 20+ years of hands-on experience at a fair, neighbor-friendly price — not bottom-of-the-barrel pricing, but real craftsmanship without the big-crew overhead. I keep my costs low because I work close to home in The Ridges, and I pass that on to my neighbors.

How quickly can you start? +

It depends on the project and the season. For smaller projects, often within a week or two of the estimate. For larger projects (basement finishes, full bath updates), the schedule is built around the scope and the materials lead time. We’ll talk through realistic timelines at the estimate.

How do I get a free estimate? +

Fill out the short form on the estimate request page and tell me about your project. I’ll reach out within 24 hours to schedule a free on-site walk-through. You can also call or text (402) 350-2583 or email hello@thehandyneighbor.co.
