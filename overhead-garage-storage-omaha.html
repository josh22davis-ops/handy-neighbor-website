Common Questions

What’s included in the price? +

Every price is a flat-rate installed price: built from solid lumber, custom sized to your space, and installed. Paint and rack add-ons are optional. Totes are not included.

Are totes included with a rack? +

No. Racks are built for standard 27-gallon totes, about 30 x 20 x 14 in. Totes are not included.

Is paint extra? +

Yes, paint is optional. On a loft, white or black is $250. A custom color is $350 if you provide the paint, or $450 if I supply it. On a tote rack, white or black is $200.

What sizes do you build? +

Lofts are built for a single 8 ft door ($550 installed), a double door ($750), or both a single and a double together ($950), each custom sized to your garage. Tote racks come in 8, 12, 16 and 20 tote sizes ($295 to $595 installed), or a custom size on request.

Can a tote rack go in the basement? +

Yes. Racks work in a garage or a basement. Add locking casters ($120) if you want it to roll.

Will it be in the way of my garage door? +

No. The loft is built above the garage door and stays out of sight when the door is open.
