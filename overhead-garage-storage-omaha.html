What’s included in the price? +

The winter sale prices are installed prices: a storage loft built from solid lumber above your garage door, custom sized to your garage, and the installation. Paint is an optional add-on.

Yes, paint is optional. White or black is $250. A custom color is $350 if you provide the paint, or $450 if I supply it.

No. The loft is built above the garage door and stays out of sight when the door is open.
